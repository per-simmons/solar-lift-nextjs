# How Solar Lift Boosted Eagle Solar & Light's Commercial Lead Quality by 74%

Eagle Solar & Light, a premier solar provider across the Southeast, had established itself as a leader in commercial and residential solar installations but faced growing challenges in generating quality leads for their expanding commercial solar division. Despite strong technical expertise and a dedicated team, their lead acquisition process wasn't scaling efficiently with their growth ambitions.

## About Eagle Solar & Light

Founded in 2016 by Sam Yates, Eagle Solar & Light has quickly become the leading commercial and industrial (C&I) solar provider in the Southeast. Headquartered in Birmingham, Alabama, with additional offices in North Carolina, the company offers turnkey solar and energy storage solutions for commercial, residential, and nonprofit clients. With installations totaling over 11MW of solar energy across multiple states, Eagle Solar & Light has built a reputation for quality installations and exceptional customer service.

## The Challenge

Despite their strong market position, Eagle Solar & Light struggled with several key challenges in their commercial lead generation:

- **High cost per acquisition**: Their existing marketing efforts were resulting in a commercial lead acquisition cost of over $1,200 per lead, straining marketing budgets
- **Quality inconsistency**: Only about 27% of incoming commercial leads met their qualification criteria for buildable projects
- **Limited targeting precision**: Their digital marketing couldn't effectively target the right decision-makers for commercial solar projects
- **Resource drain**: The sales team spent excessive time screening leads that weren't ultimately viable

"We were drowning in leads, but thirsty for quality," said Sam Yates, Founder & CEO. "Our commercial team was spending too much time qualifying prospects who either didn't own their buildings, lacked the necessary roof space, or simply weren't serious about solar investments."

## The Solar Lift Solution

After a comprehensive audit of Eagle Solar & Light's existing lead generation processes, Solar Lift implemented a multi-faceted approach:

1. **Customized pre-qualification system**: Solar Lift developed an automated pre-qualification workflow specifically for commercial solar prospects, filtering for building ownership, roof condition, and energy consumption thresholds before leads reached the sales team.

2. **Industry-specific targeting**: Rather than targeting general "solar commercial" keywords, Solar Lift created specialized ad campaigns for industries where Eagle Solar had demonstrated the strongest ROI: manufacturing facilities, warehouses, and houses of worship.

3. **Two-stage qualification process**: Solar Lift implemented a text message pre-qualification step for all incoming leads, enabling fast initial screening before deeper qualification via phone.

4. **Data-enriched lead handoffs**: When passing qualified leads to Eagle Solar's team, Solar Lift included valuable additional information like building size, ownership verification, and estimated energy usage, enabling more productive initial conversations.

## The Results

Within 90 days of implementing Solar Lift's commercial lead generation system, Eagle Solar & Light experienced dramatic improvements:

- **74% increase in commercial lead quality** with qualified leads rising from 27% to 47% of total lead volume
- **53% reduction in cost per qualified lead**, from $1,200 to $564
- **63% faster lead-to-proposal time** as the sales team focused exclusively on pre-qualified opportunities
- **28% increase in average commercial project size** as better targeting uncovered larger facilities with greater solar potential

"Solar Lift transformed our commercial lead generation from a volume game to a precision operation," said Sam Yates. "The quality of conversations our sales team is having has dramatically improved, and we're landing bigger projects with shorter sales cycles. The pre-qualification process they built has become an invaluable asset to our growth strategy."

Following their success with commercial leads, Eagle Solar & Light has expanded their partnership with Solar Lift to include residential lead generation and targeted campaigns for their battery storage solutions.
