# How Solar Lift Helped PosiGen Scale Qualified Leads While Cutting Acquisition Costs

PosiGen, the nation's leading provider of renewable energy and energy efficiency solutions for low-to-moderate income (LMI) households, faced unique challenges in scaling their lead generation efforts to match their rapid growth. Despite a mission-driven approach that had already helped over 30,000 families, their customer acquisition strategy needed refinement to support their expansion goals.

## About PosiGen

Founded in the wake of Hurricane Katrina in New Orleans, PosiGen was created with a mission to make solar energy accessible to everyone, regardless of income level or credit score. Through an innovative no-credit-check solar leasing program and energy efficiency upgrades, PosiGen helps underserved communities reduce energy costs and build a more sustainable future. Under the leadership of CEO Peter Shaper, the company has expanded beyond Louisiana to operate in multiple states, with a focus on ensuring customers save money on their monthly energy costs from day one.

## The Challenge

As PosiGen expanded into new markets, they encountered several critical obstacles in their lead generation approach:

- **Variable market awareness**: In newer territories, potential customers had limited understanding of solar leasing options, especially PosiGen's unique no-credit-check model
- **Inefficient lead qualification**: Without a standardized process, only 18% of generated leads converted to appointments, wasting valuable sales resources
- **Inconsistent messaging**: Their unique value proposition for underserved communities wasn't consistently communicated across marketing channels
- **Digital advertising inefficiency**: Despite significant ad spend, their cost per lead had climbed to over $680, with diminishing returns in newer markets

"Our mission is to make solar accessible to everyone, but our marketing approach wasn't matching that inclusivity," said Peter Shaper, CEO. "We needed a more efficient way to identify and connect with households that would truly benefit from our no-credit-check solar program while scaling our operations."

## The Solar Lift Solution

Solar Lift implemented a comprehensive lead generation transformation, focusing on PosiGen's unique value proposition for underserved communities:

1. **Educational-first approach**: Rather than leading with technical solar details, Solar Lift created simple, benefit-focused messaging emphasizing "no credit checks" and "guaranteed first-year savings" for homeowners.

2. **Advanced ZIP code targeting**: Solar Lift developed a proprietary model that identified high-potential zip codes based on homeownership rates, average utility costs, and income levels – identifying the sweet spot where PosiGen's program offered maximum benefits.

3. **Multi-channel qualification system**: Solar Lift deployed an automated text and email qualification sequence that efficiently filtered leads based on home ownership, roof condition, and energy usage patterns before scheduling sales appointments.

4. **Community-based referral automation**: Recognizing the power of community in PosiGen's model, Solar Lift built a referral automation system that made it simple for satisfied customers to refer friends and neighbors, tracking and rewarding these valuable leads.

## The Results

PosiGen saw immediate and significant improvement in their lead generation metrics within the first 60 days of implementing Solar Lift's solutions:

- **42% reduction in cost per qualified lead**, from $680 to just $394
- **137% increase in qualified lead volume** across all markets, but particularly in newer territories
- **Lead-to-appointment ratio improved from 18% to 37%**, dramatically increasing sales team efficiency
- **68% higher referral conversion rate** through the automated community referral system
- **22% increase in average contract value** as better targeting identified homes with higher energy usage

"Solar Lift fundamentally changed how we connect with potential customers," said Peter Shaper. "Their approach aligns perfectly with our mission of making solar accessible to everyone. We're now able to identify and reach the exact households who can benefit most from our program, while dramatically improving the efficiency of our marketing spend. This partnership has been instrumental in helping us bring clean, affordable energy to more underserved communities."

The success of the initial program has led PosiGen to expand Solar Lift's role to include lead generation for their energy efficiency upgrades and to support their expansion into additional states.
