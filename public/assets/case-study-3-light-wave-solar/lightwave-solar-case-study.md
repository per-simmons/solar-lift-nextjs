# How Solar Lift Revitalized LightWave Solar's Lead Generation After Incentive Changes

LightWave Solar, a pioneering solar installer in Tennessee since 2006, encountered a significant market shift when state solar incentives changed. As one of the Southeast's most experienced solar engineering, procurement, and construction (EPC) firms, they needed to quickly adapt their lead generation strategy to maintain growth in a transformed market landscape.

## About LightWave Solar

Founded in Nashville in 2006, LightWave Solar helped pioneer the solar industry in Tennessee. Led by President and CEO Chris Koczaja, the company has installed over 750 solar projects for businesses, municipalities, and homeowners across the Southeast. With the most NABCEP-certified solar professionals in the region and a focus on turnkey solar design, installation, and maintenance, LightWave Solar has established itself as a trusted provider of quality solar solutions.

## The Challenge

When Tennessee's favorable net metering policies changed, LightWave Solar faced multiple challenges that threatened their established business model:

- **Shifting value proposition**: Without strong net metering incentives, the traditional ROI-based sales approach no longer resonated with potential customers
- **Pivot to solar + storage**: The market shift required LightWave to transition toward more complex solar + battery storage systems, but lead generation hadn't adapted to this new focus
- **Higher project thresholds**: With more sophisticated, higher-cost systems, they needed to attract customers with greater purchasing power and different motivations
- **Marketing message fragmentation**: Their digital marketing still emphasized outdated benefits that no longer applied in the new regulatory environment

"There was no return on investment anymore," said Chris Koczaja, CEO of LightWave Solar. "Without net metering, without a program, we had to figure out what our approach was going to look like moving forward. We needed to completely rethink how we communicated the value of solar."

## The Solar Lift Solution

Solar Lift conducted a comprehensive audit of LightWave's position and developed a multi-faceted lead generation strategy focused on resiliency and energy independence:

1. **Resiliency-first messaging**: Solar Lift completely redesigned LightWave's marketing to emphasize energy security during increasingly common severe weather events, rather than focusing primarily on financial returns.

2. **Location-based targeting**: Using a database of recent power outages and grid reliability issues, Solar Lift created hyper-targeted campaigns for neighborhoods most affected by unreliable power.

3. **Qualification for advanced systems**: Solar Lift implemented a sophisticated lead qualification process that assessed not just solar viability but also backup power needs, critical loads, and budget alignment for more complex systems.

4. **Educational micro-campaigns**: To bridge the knowledge gap around battery storage, Solar Lift created digestible educational content sequences that guided prospects through the benefits of solar + storage systems.

## The Results

Within 120 days of implementing Solar Lift's revamped lead generation approach, LightWave Solar experienced remarkable improvements:

- **156% increase in qualified leads** for combined solar + storage systems
- **43% reduction in cost per qualified lead**, despite targeting a more affluent customer segment
- **29% increase in average project value** as customers embraced more comprehensive energy solutions
- **62% higher close rate** on qualified leads due to better alignment with the new value proposition
- **Nearly 99% of new installations included storage**, compared to less than 40% before the program

"Solar Lift completely transformed how we connect with the right customers in this new market reality," said Chris Koczaja. "Instead of mourning the loss of incentives, they helped us pivot to a more resilient, future-focused offering that actually increased our project values. Their approach recognized that while our customer demographic had shifted, there was still tremendous demand for the energy security that solar + storage provides. This partnership has been instrumental in navigating what could have been a devastating market change."

The success has led LightWave Solar to expand Solar Lift's role to include lead generation for commercial microgrid projects and to support their expansion into additional states in the Southeast.
