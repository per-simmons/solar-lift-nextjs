# How Solar Lift Helped WBE Solar Triple Qualified Lead Generation in Just 60 Days

WBE Solar, a premier commercial solar installation company based in Novato, CA, faced a common challenge in the industry: generating high-quality leads that converted into profitable installations. Despite their reputation as one of the Bay Area's leading commercial electrical contractors with extensive solar expertise, they struggled to create a consistent pipeline of qualified commercial solar prospects.

## About WBE Solar

WBE (W. Bradley Electric) has been setting industry standards in the Bay Area since 1977. Under CEO Leslie Murphy's leadership, the company expanded from a $4 million electrical contractor to a diversified operation with over $125 million in annual revenue and 300+ employees. Their solar division specializes in designing, engineering, and constructing commercial photovoltaic systems ranging from small businesses to large-scale utility solar farms.

## The Challenge

Despite their technical expertise and stellar reputation, WBE Solar faced several significant challenges:

- **Inconsistent lead quality**: Their existing marketing efforts generated plenty of inquiries, but many came from prospects with unrealistic budgets or unsuitable facilities.
- **High customer acquisition costs**: They were spending nearly $950 to acquire each new lead, with only 8% qualifying for a sales appointment.
- **Lengthy sales cycles**: Without proper qualification upfront, the sales team wasted valuable time on prospects who weren't ready to move forward.
- **Difficulty targeting the right decision-makers**: Reaching facility managers and business owners who actually had authority to approve commercial solar projects proved challenging.

"Our team was spending too much time chasing leads that weren't panning out," said Leslie Murphy, CEO. "We knew our service was top-notch, but we needed a more strategic approach to find the right commercial clients who were actually ready to invest in solar."

## The Solar Lift Solution

After analyzing WBE's existing marketing efforts, Solar Lift implemented a comprehensive lead generation strategy:

1. **Custom commercial solar qualification funnel**: Solar Lift created a multi-step qualification process specifically designed for commercial solar leads, focusing on key factors like facility ownership, roof conditions, and energy consumption.

2. **Targeted ad campaigns**: Instead of broad regional advertising, Solar Lift developed specialized campaigns for specific commercial sectors where WBE had the most success: manufacturing, warehousing, and biotech facilities.

3. **SMS-based qualification system**: Solar Lift deployed an automated text messaging system to pre-qualify leads before they reached WBE's sales team. This system filtered out prospects who weren't facility owners or lacked decision-making authority.

4. **Sales-ready handoffs**: Only after leads met strict qualification criteria did Solar Lift introduce them to WBE's sales team, complete with detailed facility information and budget parameters.

## The Results

Within 60 days of implementing Solar Lift's lead generation system, WBE Solar saw remarkable improvements:

- **68% reduction in cost per qualified lead**, from $950 to just $304
- **3x increase in qualified commercial solar leads** entering their pipeline each month
- **41% shorter sales cycle** as the team focused exclusively on pre-qualified prospects
- **24% higher average project value** as the qualification process filtered for larger commercial facilities with greater solar potential

"Solar Lift completely transformed our lead generation process," said Leslie Murphy. "Their qualification system ensures we're only talking to serious prospects who own suitable facilities and have realistic budgets. Our sales team now spends time where it matters - designing systems and closing deals, not screening unqualified leads. I only wish we'd found them sooner."

The partnership continues to drive growth for WBE Solar as they expand their commercial solar operations throughout Northern California.
